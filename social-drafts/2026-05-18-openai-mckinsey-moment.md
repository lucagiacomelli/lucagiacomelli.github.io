## LinkedIn Post

Every week, Imperial Business School circulates McKinsey consulting roles to MBA candidates.

I used to read those as a benchmark. Now I read them as a clock.

OpenAI just launched a $4B company — not to build better models, but to implement them. They embed engineers inside companies. McKinsey and Capgemini co-funded it.

When incumbents co-fund their own replacement, that's not a partnership. That's a confession.

The consulting model was built on information asymmetry and deferring implementation to someone else. AI killed the first. The FDE model is killing the second.

## Article Summary

Starting from the personal observation of McKinsey job posts in the Imperial Business School alumni network, the article argues that traditional consulting is bifurcating: high-stakes judgment advisory survives, but framework delivery and implementation advisory are being displaced by AI tools and Forward Deployed Engineer-model firms like OpenAI's new $4B Deployment Company. The piece dissects why McKinsey and Capgemini co-funding the deployment company is a defensive hedge, explains the FDE model vs. the classic consulting engagement, and ends with sharp advice for both business school students and enterprise executives.
