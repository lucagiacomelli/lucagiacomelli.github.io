Tomorrow I'm having ACL reconstruction surgery.

Over the past three weeks: 3 pre-assessment appointments, 5 clinical letters.

Almost none of them.

Turns out Kaiser Permanente already figured this out — 40 hospitals, $3.20 back per dollar invested, 14-month payback.

The clinical judgment needs a human. The letter that follows it doesn't.
