The Strait Shuts. The Algorithms Adapt.

When Iran closed the Strait of Hormuz in March 2026, 383 vessels needed new routes. Fast.

No human dispatcher could reprice 10,000 ocean edges in minutes.

An AI did.

An A* pathfinding algorithm continuously weighted by satellite AIS data, live weather feeds, and conflict-zone risk scores found the Cape of Good Hope alternative in seconds. Every 60 seconds the ensemble ML model (gradient-boosted trees + LSTM for time-series) revised edge costs as new signals arrived.

The algorithm made the call before the news broke.
