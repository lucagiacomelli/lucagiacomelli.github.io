01 / About

Former Athlete.
Engineer who speaks business.

I was a professional footballer — playing for clubs across Italy's professional leagues. I started my Computer Science Engineering degree while still playing professionally. I graduated twice — BSc and MSc — then went to Oxford to do AI research, co-authoring papers published at ACM CIKM and ISWC.

Over the decade since, I've built AI systems that actually ship: recommendation engines in mobile SDKs platforms in HealthTech and AI native products. Currently Co-Founder & CTO of an AI-native startup in San Francisco, and completing my MBA at Imperial College London.

Deep AI engineering and serious business training rarely come in the same person. I'm one of the few who has both.
